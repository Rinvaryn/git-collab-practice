One night, two friends decided to explore an abandoned house at the edge of town. They had heard strange noises coming from inside, but nobody knew what was causing them. As they opened the front door, they noticed a light flickering upstairs.

As they slowly made their way upstairs, the light suddenly disappeared. Before they could turn around, they heard footsteps coming from the room at the end of the hallway.

They slowly opened the door and discovered an old radio playing by itself. Realizing the footsteps were coming from the radio, they laighed nervously and decided it was time to leave the house.
